Description

Supply and installation of marine rubber fender systems across four berths at Port Terminal 3, including mounting hardware, corrosion-resistant fasteners, and a five-year performance warranty.

Bidders are expected to demonstrate prior experience with port infrastructure projects of comparable scale, and to comply fully with the statutory and technical requirements listed under Eligibility.
